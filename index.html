ABOUT THE PROJECT

Health & Fitness Club Membership Platform

This project focuses on managing and analyzing health and fitness club membership operations using a CRM / MIS approach.


The platform organizes member information, membership plans, payment history and attendance data. Its analytical dashboard provides insights into revenue, membership distribution, engagement and churn risk to support business decisions.
