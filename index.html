ABOUT THE PROJECT

Health & Fitness Club Membership Platform

This project focuses on managing and analyzing health and fitness club membership operations using a CRM / MIS approach.


The platform organizes member information, membership plans and fitness activity attributes. Its analytical dashboard presents membership distribution, modelled revenue, engagement categories and estimated churn risk to support business decision-making.
