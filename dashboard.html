ABOUT THE PROJECT

Health & Fitness Club Membership Platform

This project focuses on managing and analyzing health and fitness club membership operations. It uses a CRM / MIS approach to organize member information, membership plans, payment history and attendance-related business data.


The analytics dashboard provides insights into membership distribution, revenue performance, payment methods, member engagement and churn risk. These insights support better business decisions and operational planning.
