Data Notes & Limitations

Transparency about the demonstration dataset

1. Sample data: This dashboard uses 1,000 illustrative member records.

2. Plan allocation: IDs 1–500 are assigned Premium and IDs 501–1000 are assigned Standard for demonstration purposes.

3. Gender: Aggregate dataset counts are Female 503 and Male 497. Individual gender mapping is not available here.

4. Engagement: Engagement distribution uses the supplied aggregate figures: Moderate 586, High 220 and Low 194.

5. Churn Risk: Churn risk distribution uses the supplied aggregate figures: Low 532, Medium 274 and High 194.

6. Revenue: ₹40 lakh is a modeled sample total based on the illustrative plan allocation and sample payments.

7. Connectivity: This is a static front-end demonstration. It is not connected to a live MySQL database and does not process real payments or save edits.
