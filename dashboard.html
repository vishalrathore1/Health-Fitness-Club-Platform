Data and methodology notes
Member profile, age, gender, membership type, visit frequency, group lessons, average gym time, drink subscription, personal training and sauna fields come from the uploaded CSV. The monthly revenue card is a modeled estimate using Premium ₹5,000 and Standard ₹3,000 monthly fees. Payment status, payment method, membership validity and historical attendance transactions are not included in this CSV and are not presented as member-level facts here. Estimated churn risk is based on visit frequency only.
